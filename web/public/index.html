What the plan needs

Who pledged

Each council voted to file its own transfer proposal to Lockstep: that proposal is the pledge. Until settlement, the money stays in each DAO's treasury.

    What completes it

    The top-up is held by the contract and returned if the plan does not settle. Demo donor: .

    Try the failure. Before activating, let Community DAO's council pay 250 tUSD for something else. Its pledge stays on record, but its treasury no longer holds it.

    Settle, or roll back

    Anyone may call each step. The contract decides; this page only presses the buttons.

    1. The contract checks that every line is covered exactly, within each pledge's cap and permitted lines.
    2. For each DAO, Lockstep approves the DAO's own transfer proposal, keeps the share and sends the rest straight back. If any share fails, all of it goes back.
    3. Payouts to the payees, or refunds to every DAO and donor that had sent money.
